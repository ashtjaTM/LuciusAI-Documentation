# Login and Sign-up Flows

## Welcome to Lucius AI

Before you can begin creating and executing AI-powered test cases, you must have an active Lucius AI account. Whether you're joining as a new user, accepting an invitation from your organization, or returning to an existing workspace, Lucius AI provides a streamlined authentication process to help you get started securely.

This guide walks you through every authentication scenario supported by Lucius AI, including:

- Account creation
- Email verification
- Signing in
- Accepting workspace invitations
- Recovering your password

By the end of this guide, you'll be able to access your Lucius AI workspace and begin using the platform.

# Creating a New Account

If you're using Lucius AI for the first time, you'll need to create a new account before accessing the platform.

### Steps

1. Open the **Lucius AI Sign Up** page.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Create a secure password that meets the required password criteria.
4. Confirm your password.
5. Select **Create Account**.

Once your information has been submitted successfully, Lucius AI creates your account and sends a verification email to the registered email address.

# Verifying Your Email Address

To protect your account and confirm ownership of your email address, Lucius AI requires email verification before allowing access to the platform.

### Steps

1. Open your inbox.
2. Locate the verification email sent by Lucius AI.
3. Select **Verify Email**.
4. Wait while your account is activated.

After successful verification, Lucius AI automatically continues with your onboarding journey. You can proceed ahead with Lucius AI in **one of two ways**:

1. **Subscribe to a plan** and set up your own organization.
2. **Join an existing organization** if you have been invited to one.

# Selecting a Subscription Plan

If you choose to set up your own organization, Lucius AI displays the available subscription plans on the **Select a plan to continue** screen. Choose the plan that best matches your requirements to proceed with onboarding.

### Available Plans

### Free

The **Free** plan is designed for getting started with Lucius AI and is available for a trial period of **15 days**. It includes:

- **Auto + Manual** testing modes
- **GitHub** integration
- **Runners**
- **Bulk Import** of test cases

Select **Start testing free** to continue subscribing with the Free plan.

#### Enterprise

The **Enterprise** plan is designed for teams and organizations and includes everything available in the Free plan, along with additional capabilities such as:

- Custom usage limits
- Teams and multi-organization capabilities
- SSO and advanced security features
- Tunnel and Browser Profiles capabilities
- Dedicated customer support

To proceed with Enterprise, select **Talk to us** to contact the Lucius AI team.

# Joining an Existing Workspace

If you've already been invited by an administrator or workspace owner, Lucius AI automatically detects the pending invitation after your account has been verified.

The invitation page displays information about the workspace you've been invited to join.

You can choose one of the following options.

## Accept Invitation

Selecting **Accept** immediately adds your account to the invited workspace and redirects you to the Lucius AI Dashboard.

You'll be able to access all projects and resources according to the permissions assigned by your administrator.

## Decline Invitation

If you choose **Decline**, the invitation is rejected.

Since no workspace is associated with your account, Lucius AI continues with the standard onboarding process by asking you to select a subscription plan and create your own workspace.

# Resetting Your Password

If you've forgotten your password or are unable to sign in to your Lucius AI account, you can securely reset it using your registered email address.

### Steps

1. Open the Lucius AI Sign In page.
2. Enter the email address associated with your Lucius AI account.
3. Select **Continue**.
4. When prompted to enter your password, select **Reset Password**.
5. A password reset email is sent to your registered email address.
6. Open the password reset email and select **Reset Password**.
7. You are redirected to the password reset page.
8. Enter your new password. Ensure it meets the required password criteria.
9. Select **Continue** to save your new password.
10. Return to the Lucius AI Sign In page.
11. Enter your email address, select **Continue**, and sign in using your new password.

## Next Steps

Once you've successfully signed in, you're ready to begin using Lucius AI. Continue with the onboarding process to configure your workspace, create projects, and start building AI-powered test cases.
